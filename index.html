        This app reads class QR codes and, when you scan, checks whether you are on campus. It keeps no exact location, contacts or photos.

        Location at scan

        iPhone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services: on, and Safari Websites › While Using the App. Android: in Chrome open the app address, tap the icon left of the address › Permissions › Location › Allow; Chrome itself also needs Location (Settings › Apps › Chrome › Permissions).

        The app checks your position once, when you scan a class code, and only when MyFiles checks location for your department. The position is sent with that scan and deleted from the phone as soon as MyFiles answers. MyFiles keeps only “on campus” or “not on campus”. With location off, your instructor checks your attendance.

        Location at scan

        MyFiles checks your location only at the moment you scan, to see whether you are on the CarSU campus. MyFiles keeps only “on campus” or “not on campus”, never where you were. You can say no; your instructor will then check your attendance.
